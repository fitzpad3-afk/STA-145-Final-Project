# STA-145-Final-Project
My replication package for my STA-145 final project. I took the discography of Spotify's Billboard Top 100 songs and analyzed the lengths, artist descriptions, and the songs' genres, which album each is on, as well as how much individuals enjoyed each song. I sought to figure out if I enjoyed a certain type of song more than others and which album I rated the highest statistically 


# Instructions

To access and utilize the script, open the "STA145-FITZPAD3.R" file, which contains the code for my project. To access the dataset, open the "FITZPAD3-Final_Project - Sheet1 (2).csv" file.


# Data 

The data collected for this project was the top 100 songs on Spotify's Billboard charts. The goal of the analysis was to look at popular songs and find similarities in the music. I collected information about each song, including its title, artist, number of streams, song length, and other details. I used this information to correlate the songs and see what they have in common and what makes songs more popular than others.


# Operationalization

To operationalize the age of the artists, I went to Google and looked up the age of each artist on Spotify's top 100 songs on the Billboard charts.

I did the same to find whether the song is a pop song or not, going to Spotify and checking to see if the song is pop and analyzing it.

The data collected for each song was put into 2 different categories: the artist's gender and the release year of a song. Each artist was put into a category as either male or female based on the artist performing the song. The release year was logged to show when each song was released. These categories helped correlate the songs and to look for similar patterns between the artists and the years their songs were released

The duration of each song was measured by a song's length in seconds. Each song's length was collected and compared to see which song was longer than another. This data helped identify the lengths of songs and helped determine whether the top 100 Spotify Billboard songs were short, medium, or long in duration. 

I measured my personal enjoyment on a scale of 1-10, with 1 meaning I disliked the song and 10 meaning I loved the song.

My final variable was the album each song was on. This was used as a grouping variable to measure the differences between not only the individual songs, but the albums as a whole.
